# Development Research & Data Intelligence Portfolio

A multidisciplinary portfolio exploring development challenges across **health, the economy, and education** through data analysis, research, and evidence-based reporting.

The portfolio brings together different forms of data—including survey, operational, financial, administrative, and service-delivery data—and examines them at individual, household, institutional, community, and national levels.

## Portfolio focus

| Pillar | Research focus | Example questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | Healthcare access, facility readiness, service delivery, hospital operations, and health financing | Are facilities equipped to deliver quality care? How do operational and financial patterns inform hospital management? |
| **Economy** | Financial inclusion, digital access, businesses, livelihoods, and markets | How is digital access associated with financial inclusion? What patterns appear in business performance and market activity? |
| **Education** | Education access, enrolment, learning outcomes, school resources, and financing | Where do access and learning gaps occur? How are school resources and expenditure distributed? |

## Data perspectives

Projects may use one or more of these data categories:

1. **Survey data** — responses from individuals, households, businesses, workers, patients, or institutions.
2. **Operational data** — records generated through routine organisational activities.
3. **Financial data** — revenue, expenditure, budgets, payments, costs, and financial transactions.
4. **Administrative data** — institutional registers, enrolment records, staffing records, and government datasets.
5. **Service-delivery and outcome data** — measures of availability, readiness, quality, performance, and results.

The unit of analysis will be stated for each project. Depending on the research question, this may be an individual, household, facility, school, business, community, or country.

## Research and analytical workflow

Each project will follow a documented workflow appropriate to its data and research question:

1. **Research background** — define the development issue and why it matters.
2. **Research objectives and questions** — specify what the analysis will investigate.
3. **Data source and methodology** — document provenance, collection, sampling, coverage, and limitations.
4. **Data dictionary** — define variables, categories, units, and measurement.
5. **Data quality assessment** — examine completeness, validity, consistency, duplicates, and missing values.
6. **Data preparation** — clean, standardise, and structure the data.
7. **Data management** — use SQL and PostgreSQL where appropriate.
8. **Analysis** — apply descriptive statistics and suitable analytical methods.
9. **Visualisation** — communicate results through clear charts and Power BI dashboards where useful.
10. **Interpretation and recommendations** — distinguish observed patterns from explanations and causal claims.

## Tools and methods

Tools will be selected according to the project rather than forced into every analysis.

- **Excel and Power Query** — inspection, transformation, and data preparation.
- **Python** — data cleaning, exploration, and statistical analysis.
- **SQL and PostgreSQL** — relational data management and querying.
- **Power BI and DAX** — interactive reporting and visualisation.
- **Git and GitHub** — version control, documentation, and reproducibility.

## Research standards

- Identify the original source of every external dataset and provide its citation or download link.
- Respect dataset licences, terms of use, privacy requirements, and disclosure restrictions.
- Never publish personally identifiable, confidential, or sensitive records.
- Clearly label simulated, synthetic, or practice data.
- Do not present planned work as completed work.
- Document methods, assumptions, missing data, and limitations.
- Treat statistical associations as associations unless a suitable research design supports causal inference.
- Make analysis reproducible wherever data access and privacy conditions permit.
