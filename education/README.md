# Education Research & Data Analysis

This section focuses on education access, enrolment, attendance, learning outcomes, school resources, infrastructure, and education financing.

## Projects

| ID | Project | Data focus | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ED1 | Education Access & Enrolment | Household survey, enrolment, attendance, school records | Planned |
| ED2 | Learning Outcomes & Educational Performance | Literacy, numeracy, assessment outcomes | Planned |
| ED3 | School Infrastructure & Education Financing | Facilities, staffing, budgets, expenditure | Planned |

## Research approach

Each project will define its population and unit of analysis, document the data source and methodology, assess data quality, and interpret findings within the limits of the available evidence.
